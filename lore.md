# Necrogrid Lore: The Phylactery

**Status:** Draft, from the design discussion. Lore is flavor. Mechanics live in GAME_DESIGN.md.

## What the Phylactery Is

The Phylactery is the binding the necromancer is building at the heart of the wasteland. It is the reason the base exists. Every wall, totem, and lantern is built to keep the binding safe while it is finished.

It sits at the center of the map, a 3×3 vessel of dark working that the demons are drawn to. They come for it as surely as they come for the necromancer.

## Why It Matters

Finishing the Phylactery binds the necromancer's soul to it. Once bound, death no longer ends them. They wake again at the Phylactery, whole and ready, with their gear and inventory intact. This is a form of lich-like immortality.

Until then the necromancer is mortal, and the demons know it. Destroying the Phylactery unravels the binding and ends the run, whether or not the necromancer is still standing.

## Fifteen Days of Work

The binding is built over all fifteen days. The necromancer works on it from the first dawn, and every day adds to the working. Nothing visibly changes on the vessel until the final day, when the binding reaches its last stage and closes.

Demons pour in through the day, and each night the Wraiths come. Those spirits drift in from nowhere, straight at the Phylactery, because they are tied to the same working that the necromancer is trying to finish. They come every night, not just the last one.

The necromancer cannot simply run away and wait out the clock. The Phylactery has to be defended in person, every night, for all fifteen days.

## Win and Loss, in Lore Terms

- **Completion (the win):** the binding closes on day fifteen. The necromancer is now undying, and the run continues.
- **Destruction (the loss):** the vessel shatters. The soul is lost, and the run ends.
- **Death before completion:** the necromancer's soul is not yet bound. Death ends the run.
- **Death after completion:** the necromancer rises again at the Phylactery.

## Notes for Future Writing

- Keep the tone grim and practical. The necromancer is a builder working against a deadline, not a villain gloating.
- Demons are mindless pressure. They are not sentient enemies with motives.
- Any new lore about allies, raised minions, or other necromantic practice is out of scope for v1 (see GAME_DESIGN.md section 17).
